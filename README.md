# Excel-Project
# Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard — Monthly Report

## Recommended Structure and Order

### 1. Project Title / Headline

**Hospital Emergency Room Analytics: Patient Flow, Waiting Time & Satisfaction Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor emergency room patient visits, waiting times, admission status, patient satisfaction, gender distribution, and department referrals.

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard is a business intelligence solution developed to analyze hospital emergency department performance through monthly and yearly patient data. It provides insights into patient attendance, average waiting time, satisfaction scores, admission rates, and department referral patterns.

The dashboard helps healthcare administrators monitor operational efficiency, understand patient flow, and identify opportunities to improve waiting times and patient experience.

### 3. Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard creation, KPI cards, charts, and interactive reports.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Calculating patient counts, average waiting time, satisfaction scores, and admission percentages.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection and preparation, if used in the project.

### 4. Dataset Overview

The dashboard presents emergency room patient data with dimensions including:

- Patient visit dates and months
- Patient IDs
- Waiting time and attendance status
- Admission status
- Gender
- Age groups
- Department referrals
- Patient satisfaction scores

### 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays three primary KPIs:

1. **Number of Patients:** 469
2. **Average Wait Time:** 35.04
3. **Patient Satisfaction Score:** 4.63

### 6. Dashboard Visualizations

**A. Patient Attendance Within Time**
- Pie chart showing 57% of patients attended within the expected time and 43% classified as delayed.
- Helps monitor emergency room service timeliness.

**B. Patient Count by Gender**
- Donut chart comparing female and male patients.
- Displays approximately 51% female and 49% male patients.

**C. Admission Status**
- Compares admitted and non-admitted patients.
- Admitted: 236 patients (51%).
- Not admitted: 228 patients (49%).

**D. Patients by Age Group**
- Column chart comparing patient visits across age groups.
- The 20–29 age group has the highest displayed patient count at 69.

**E. Patients by Department Referral**
- Horizontal bar chart showing patient referrals by department.
- The largest category is “None,” followed by General Practice and Orthopedics.

**F. Monthly and Yearly Filters**
- Month slicer for January through December.
- Year selection for 2023 and 2024.
- Enables users to explore patient data across selected reporting periods.

### 7. Key Business Insights

- The dashboard reports 469 patients for the selected reporting context.
- The average waiting time is 35.04, with the time unit requiring confirmation from the dataset.
- The patient satisfaction score is 4.63, indicating a relatively high score on the dashboard's scale.
- Approximately 57% of patients were attended to within the expected time, while 43% experienced delays.
- Admission rates are nearly evenly split, with 51% admitted and 49% not admitted.
- Patients aged 20–29 form the largest age group in the displayed age distribution.
- The referral chart shows a large number of visits categorized as “None,” which may warrant review of referral-data completeness and categorization.

### 8. Business Recommendations

- Investigate causes of emergency room delays and identify opportunities to improve patient flow.
- Monitor waiting time and attendance performance by month and year.
- Review staffing and resource allocation during periods of higher patient demand.
- Analyze patient satisfaction alongside waiting time to identify potential service improvements.
- Review referral documentation to understand why many patient visits fall under “None.”
- Compare admission patterns across age groups and reporting periods to support operational planning.

### 9. Business Impact

The dashboard consolidates patient volume, waiting time, admission status, satisfaction, and referral data into a single interactive report. It helps hospital administrators identify operational trends, monitor service performance, and make data-informed decisions to improve emergency department efficiency and patient experience.
